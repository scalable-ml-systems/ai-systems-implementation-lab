## PCIe Link

The GPU reports PCIe Gen 3 x16 as both its current and maximum
exposed link configuration. Therefore, the low pageable-memory
host↔device bandwidth observed in the initial benchmark is not
explained by a downtrained PCIe generation or reduced lane width.
Further experiments will compare pinned versus pageable host memory
and use repeated timed iterations to investigate host-side staging
and VM-platform effects.
